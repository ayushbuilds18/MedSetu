OUR PROMISE

Safety is not optional.

MedSetu helps you ask better questions. It does not make treatment decisions for you.

✚
PLEASE READ

Never switch a prescribed medicine based on price alone.

Only a doctor or qualified pharmacist who understands your prescription and health context can confirm whether a change is appropriate.

01

Ingredients matter

Brand names that sound alike can contain different ingredients. Exact composition and salt form must be checked.

02

Strength and form matter

Tablet, capsule, liquid, release type and dose are not automatically interchangeable—even when names look similar.

03

Some medicines need extra care

For medicines where consistency or close monitoring is important, ask your prescriber before any brand change.

04

Uncertainty is a valid result

If details are missing, conflicting or low-confidence, the correct next step is to pause and ask a professional.

How to read a result

Potential match

Information appears aligned in the available dataset. This is not permission to switch; verify with a professional.

Ask a professional

More context or professional judgement is needed. Do not change anything until confirmed.

Do not switch

The available information flags a mismatch or concern. Do not use the suggested item as a replacement.

Not enough data

The app cannot reliably establish a match. Use the prescription and ask a doctor or pharmacist.

ⓘ

Emergency: If you think you are having a severe allergic reaction or another medical emergency, seek urgent medical care. Do not rely on MedSetu for emergency decisions.
